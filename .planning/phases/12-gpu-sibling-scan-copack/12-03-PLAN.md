<objective>
Add a co-pack ON/OFF A/B to `bench_gpu_vs_cpu` that confirms (SC-3) the per-tree
`scan_resident` sync count ~halves (~59 -> ~30) under `LGBM_PHASE_PROF=1` on the eligible
resident path, and (SC-4) the median GPU train at small/medium is NOT SLOWER (and trends
faster, ~10-15% target) with co-pack on, with wide unaffected and routing unchanged --
reported HONESTLY per the cold-isolated-overstates-warm rule (the spike-024 isolated 2x is
NOT claimed as e2e).

Purpose: SC-3 is the structural deliverable (the sync-floor halving is empirical, shape-
independent, the 023 counters make it directly observable). SC-4 is the sign-only e2e check
on the spoofed 8-CU APU: co-packing reclaims ~half the genuine scan-sync time (spike-023:
~27-29% of train at small/medium -> ~13-14% e2e ceiling), which nudges (does NOT flip) the
CPU-vs-GPU crossover. The verification MUST NOT gate on an e2e speedup >= some threshold (the
APU is Amdahl-capped and sign-confounded); it gates on the sync-count drop + not-slower +
honest reporting.

Output: a runnable `--features rocm` A/B (`LGBM_SIBLING_COPACK=0` vs `=1`) printing per-regime
`scan_resident` sync counts (the ~halving) + median train ON vs OFF + a not-slower/trends-faster
verdict, with a header instructing >=2 process runs (sign-stability) and a closing honest
diagnosis (no e2e-2x claim).
</objective>
