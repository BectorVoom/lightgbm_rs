<objective>
Implement the §11 on-device score updater (ODL-16): two whole-array scalar `#[cube]` kernels
(`AddScoreConstant` = `score[i] += val`, `MultiplyScoreConstant` = `score[i] *= val`) over the
resident `double` score buffer at `offset = num_data * tree_id`, plus the boosting-layer
host-mirror toggle. The per-leaf `AddScore` delegates to the already-golden Phase-18
`add_prediction_to_score_on_device` (D-02 — do NOT rebuild a tree-walk score kernel).

Purpose: the resident cumulative score is the boosting-layer device path's backbone; the
constant ops serve init-score / no-split single-leaf (add) and shrinkage / DART rescale / RF
running-average (multiply). This plan delivers and unit-anchors the kernels + host toggle in
isolation; the full cross-iteration residency A/B is Plan 04 (needs the driver).
Output: `kernels/score_updater.rs` with the two launchers, the `boosting_on_cuda_` toggle in
`lgbm-boosting/score_updater.rs`, and a kernel-level parity test.
</objective>
