<objective>
Implement the §12 / §12.1 on-device pointwise metric evaluator (ODL-17): a comptime-generic
`EvalKernel<CUDA_METRIC, USE_WEIGHTS>` (recommended over 12 concrete kernels) whose per-row
`metric_on_point(label, score, param, #[comptime] metric)` transcribes the 12-row §12.1 table,
folded to `sum_loss` / `sum_weight` by the REUSED ordered f64 fold (`reduce_sum_f64_on`, D-10),
with the correct `AverageLoss` finalizers (RMSE `sqrt`, Gamma-deviance `×2`, else Σloss/Σweight).
ConvertOutput is composed into the flow this phase (D-04): inverse-link into a pre-allocated
`score_convert_buffer_` then `EvalKernel`. Anchor every metric to its real `lib_lightgbm` golden
(D-03), consuming Plan 00's captured goldens.

Purpose: completes the boosting-layer device metric path; the discriminator from Plan 00 routes
the unsupported metrics to host.
Output: `kernels/metric_pointwise.rs` + on-device parity cells in `metric_parity.rs`.
</objective>
