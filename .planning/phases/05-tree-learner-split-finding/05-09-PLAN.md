<objective>
Close the last residual on the most_freq_bin>0 real-binary learner gate: the node-2 leaf-0 leaf_value differs from the real `lib_lightgbm` 4.6 golden by exactly one f64 ULP (Rust `0.59999999999999976` vs golden `0.59999999999999953`, Δ 2.3e-16). Decoded, the golden requires `best_sum_left_hessian = 2.0000000000000013` while Rust produces `2.000000000000001` — a 2-ULP difference at the 2.0 scale. Every other field on the mfb>0 corpus is already bit-exact (split_feature, threshold incl. the zero-sentinel `1.0000000180025095e-35`, decision_type=2 2 2, child topology, leaf_count, internal_count, and 3 of 4 leaf values).

This is a SHARP, single-defect numerical-parity fix. The root cause was established by 05-07 (do NOT relitigate): leaf 0 is node-2's DIRECTLY-BUILT smaller child, so the subtraction trick never touches it. The divergence is a 2-ULP f64 accumulation/fold-order subtlety in the FixHistogram-active DIRECT histogram build — the chain `construct_histograms` (histogram.rs ordered f64 fold) → `FixHistogram` most_freq_bin reconstruction (fix_histogram.rs subtractive loop) → the REVERSE-scan `sum_right_hessian` accumulation that feeds `best_sum_left_hessian` and thus the Newton leaf output. The mfb>0 corpus is the ONLY one affected because most_freq_bin>0 is what activates FixHistogram (the spine's most_freq_bin==0 makes FixHistogram a no-op, which is why the spine is already bit-exact).

Two approaches are RULED OUT and MUST NOT be re-attempted as the primary fix (05-07 proved both):
1. Subtraction-trick / HistogramPool wiring — done in 05-07; proven NOT to touch leaf 0.
2. SplitInfo-sum `LeafSplits` seeding (serial_tree_learner.cpp:850-870 / leaf_splits.hpp:47-54) — 05-07 trialed it; it did NOT change leaf 0 and REGRESSED leaf 3.

The fix targets the construct/FixHistogram f64 FOLD ORDER and the exact float values flowing into the REVERSE scan.

Purpose: Satisfy the non-negotiable ≤1e-12 numerical-fidelity contract bit-exact on the mfb>0 real-binary corpus, completing Phase 5's keystone learner against the real oracle on BOTH committed corpora.
Output: An aligned FixHistogram-active direct-build f64 fold; the `learner_parity_mfb_pos_real_binary` gate un-#[ignore]d and passing bit-exact; the spine gate and kernel_parity unchanged.
</objective>
