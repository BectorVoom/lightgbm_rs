<objective>
Complete the 3-stage pipeline: stage-2 cross-feature reduce per leaf
(`SyncBestSplitForLeafKernel` + `…AllBlocks` fold + `SetInvalidLeafSplitInfoKernel`) and
stage-3 cross-leaf argmax + export (`FindBestFromAllSplitsKernel` +
`PrepareLeafBestSplitInfo`), producing the chosen `(leaf, feature, threshold, default_left)`
in the single 8-int buffer — the ONLY device→host transfer per iteration (SC#2). Then close
SC#3: tie-aware `default_left` parity on hip (mandatory — do NOT defer, ROADMAP).

Purpose: SC#2 + SC#3 (ODL-12). The reduction family order (`ReduceBestGainWarp`→`…Block`→
`ReduceBestGain`; stage-3 `ReduceBestGainForLeaves`) is the order the anchor pins (D-06);
stage-3 self-invalidation (chosen leaf + freshly-created leaf slot) is behavioral and must be
preserved. The 8-int export feeds Phase 18 (`CUDATree.Split` → `DataPartition.Split`).

Output: stage-2 + stage-3 kernels + the 8-int `PrepareLeafBestSplitInfo` export in
`best_split.rs`, and the extended `rocm_backend_parity.rs` `default_left_tie` test. Anchored
to the cpu f64 fold, never GPU-vs-GPU (def-f8u-01, D-08); additive, env-gated (D-09);
`on_device_growth_supported()` stays false.
</objective>
