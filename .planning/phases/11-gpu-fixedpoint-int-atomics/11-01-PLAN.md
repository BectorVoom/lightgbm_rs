<objective>
Replace the ROCm resident histogram BUILD's f32-atomic accumulation with wide fixed-point
u64 two's-complement integer atomics (scale S = 2^30), confined to the RAW-build -> dequant
seam. Quantize `round(value * 2^30)` as i64, store its BITS as u64, wrapping
`Atomic<u64>::fetch_add` (== two's-complement i64 add), and dequantize `(bits as i64) / 2^30`
to f64 inside the existing `fix_compact_kernel` widen pass. Everything downstream of the
dequant (FixHistogram fold, compact, subtract trick, scan) stays f64 and is UNCHANGED.

Purpose: The f32 `atomicAdd` on RDNA lowers to a CAS retry loop (`ds_cmpst`) that saturates
under contention; integer `ds_add_u64` is a native single-instruction op. Spike 018/019
validated ~1.3-1.7x faster build in the wide large-leaf regime, ~3600x better accuracy
(5.9e-9 vs f32 2.2e-5 rel-to-anchor; exact in the cancelling regime), and a deterministic
GPU histogram -- all within the ~1e-6 ROCm gate.

Output: a new u64 build kernel, u64 RAW + merge buffers, a u64->f64 dequant in the widen
pass, a documented overflow guard, and the updated seam doc. The CPU f64 anchor is untouched.
</objective>
