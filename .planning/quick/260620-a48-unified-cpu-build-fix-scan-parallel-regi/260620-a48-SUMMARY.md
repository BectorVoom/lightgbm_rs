## A/B Results (Task 3 — verbatim)

cubecl-cpu (f64 anchor), warm (cold iteration discarded), 3-run medians, 20 000 rows × 128 bins.
Decisive metric = warm end-to-end train-wall (`bench_train`). A = two-step default (unified OFF). B = unified forced on (`LGBM_UNIFIED_BFS_THRESHOLD=0`).

| config | A (two-step) | B (unified) | delta train-wall |
|--------|-------------|-------------|------------------|
| narrow (10 feat) | 169 ms (162.6/169.3/172.8) | 246 ms (244.5/246.3/246.7) | **+45% WORSE** (sign-stable) |
| wide (120 feat) | 810 ms (800.6/810.5/821.6) | 755 ms (754.7/755.1/762.7) | **-6.8% WIN** (sign-stable, no overlap) |

Phase counters (`bench_split_scan`, 255 bins; in mode B build+fix+scan fuse into SCAN_NS, BUILD_NS=0):

| config | A build+scan | B fused | A warm_wall | B warm_wall |
|--------|-------------|---------|-------------|-------------|
| narrow | 61.4+34.5 = 95.9 ms | 124.9 ms (+30%) | 166.7 ms | 217.9 ms (+31%) |
| wide   | 431.2+348.5 = 779.7 ms | 569.7 ms (**-27%**) | 1049.9 ms | 910.7 ms (**-13%**) |

Crossover sweep (train-wall, median of 3, 128 bins): +34.6% @20 feat -> +5.9% @40 -> +0.6% @80 (within spread) -> overlapping/sign-flips @90 -> **-5% @100 (no overlap)** -> **-6% @120 (no overlap)**. B becomes sign-stable-below-A only at >=100 feat.

**Mechanism:** fusing build+fix+compact+scan into ONE rayon fork/join (vs the two-step's TWO) keeps each feature's histogram cache-hot in its building thread, removing the 9cp two-region contention. At wide that is -27% on the hot region; at narrow the single fork/join overhead at 10 features dominates (8v4/9cp narrow-is-catastrophic lesson).

**Verdict — CONDITIONAL WIN.** Both adoption gates met: wide train-wall sign-stable gain across all 3 runs AND no narrow regression at the tuned threshold. Tuned default `unified_bfs_threshold()` = **100** (keyed on `feats.len()`): narrow/medium (<=90 feat) keep the byte-unchanged serial two-step (zero regression); genuinely wide leaves (>=100 feat) take the unified region for the -6% gain. Default-path verification (threshold=100 baked): narrow 167-179 ms (== mode A); wide 748-776 ms warm (== mode B win). Env: `LGBM_UNIFIED_BFS_THRESHOLD=0` forces unified on (parity proof); large value forces serial off.

This is the first NON-NULL of the sibling campaign — 8v4 (per-call 2-lane, ~13× slower) and 9cp (cross-region scan contention, +11% train-wall) were both honest NULLs. The difference: this lever REMOVES a fork/join (fuses two regions into one) rather than ADDING one, so it recovers locality instead of paying contention.
