REGIME DESIGN (honor CONTEXT: drive the LAUNCH-BOUND regime + at least one compute-bound contrast):
  - **Launch-bound cells** — SMALL leaf, MINIMAL per-call transfer, MANY launches. Use a small
    leaf (e.g. ~1k–4k rows) and a small feature count so the kernel does little work per launch
    and the fixed per-launch cost (incl. any bounds-check codegen in the scatter) is the largest
    relative share. Crank `TIMED` higher here (e.g. 15–21) since each call is cheap and we want
    a tight median. Bin sweep `[16, 64, 256]`.
  - **Compute-bound contrast cell** — LARGE leaf (~200k rows, FEATS=50) at 256 bins, so the
    scatter loop dominates and any launch/codegen delta is (per mwr) expected to be masked.

checked-then-unchecked launches (not all-checked-then-all-unchecked) so thermal/clock drift hits
both arms equally; discard the first WARMUP iterations of each arm; record per-call wall time
with a device sync FORCED INSIDE the timed call (read back `out[0]` / `read_one_unchecked`, exactly
as the existing harnesses do). Report median_checked, median_unchecked, the spread (min/max or
p25/p75) of each arm, and `delta% = (checked - unchecked) / checked * 100` with a sign.

    Unchecked arm → `construct_leaf_hist_resident_lds_kernel::launch_unchecked(...)`; checked arm →
    `construct_leaf_hist_resident_lds_kernel_checked::launch(...)`. Keep P=1 (the small/mid-leaf gate)

    two arms must produce BIT-IDENTICAL hist+out.
