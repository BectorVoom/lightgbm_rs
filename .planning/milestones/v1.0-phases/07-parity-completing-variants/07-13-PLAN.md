"future 07-01-style learner-level split-gain FP-trace fix plan" that

debug FP trace IS the research, so there is no research task.

Root cause (verified, `.planning/debug/split-gain-knife-edge-07-02.md`): for
`most_freq_bin == 0` features (offset=1) the Rust learner PHYSICALLY compacts the
histogram (`compact_histogram` shifts real bin `c+1 → c` and DROPS real bin 0;
`fix_histogram` is a documented no-op for mfb==0 at fix_histogram.rs:54). C++
NEVER physically compacts — `Dataset::FixHistogram` (dataset.cpp:1488-1506) only
reconstructs the most-freq cell when `most_freq_bin > 0`; for mfb==0 it keeps the
FULL buffer and the scan (range `t = num_bin-1-offset .. 1-offset`) carries the
bin-0 (most_freq) mass as the implicit `sum_total − Σ(scanned)` default. On a
small NON-CONSTANT-HESSIAN leaf (gamma tree-0 node{original rows 2,3}, row3
f1=0==most_freq_bin) Rust mis-places per-bin g/h → split net_gain 1.0417 vs C++
0.0333; the bogus gain beats the correct sibling (0.375479), flipping tree-0
topology leaf_count [1,8,2,1] vs golden [2,4,2,4]. Constant-hessian families are
unaffected (the bin-0-default placement cancels in g²/(h+λ) + integer count
gates) — which is why binary/poisson/spine stay bit-exact and only
gamma/fair/tweedie-bfa-off/quantile-iterated fail.

Purpose: un-defer the 13 Family-A cells so OBJ-04 stops being PARTIAL, WITHOUT
regressing the bit-exact CPU f64-fold merge gate.
Output: a faithful most_freq_bin==0/offset histogram model in
crates/lgbm-treelearner (kept consistent across the f64 and f32 split.rs
consumers), the 13 cells un-#[ignore]d asserting real-binary parity, and
DEF-07-02/03 cleared.

Scope is Family A ONLY. Family B (DEF-07-11: monotone_mixed, forced_nested,
extra_trees_seed6/9 in learner_parity.rs) is OUT OF SCOPE — do not touch its 4
ignored cells.
